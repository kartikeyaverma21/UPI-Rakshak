Core Rule: Entering your UPI PIN always authorizes an outgoing payment or mandate.

You never need to enter your UPI PIN or scan a QR code just to receive money. Official banks, UPI apps, and police will never ask for your PIN, OTP, or remote screen access.

NATIONAL CYBER FINANCIAL HELPLINE
1930

Report unauthorized financial transactions as quickly as possible so banks can trace and flag the transfer.

Immediate Checklist

  1. Call 1930 immediately and file a report on the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal.
  2. Contact your bank using its official app or verified helpline number to block cards/UPI and freeze compromised channels.
  3. Uninstall any remote-access apps (such as AnyDesk, TeamViewer, or QuickSupport) and change your UPI PIN and banking passwords.
  4. Save transaction reference numbers (UTR), UPI IDs, phone numbers, SMS texts, and screenshots.
  5. Ignore anyone contacting you claiming they can recover lost money for a fee—that is a follow-up scam.
